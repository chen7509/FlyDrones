# PX4 odometry causal adapter for a future student-capture producer

## Purpose

The checked EGO v3 recorder currently accepts caller-supplied labels saying that odometry came from PX4 EKF2 and that the camera is calibrated. The retained development flight has eight coordinate/attitude reset transitions, so a label or a stable later counter cannot establish a usable training frame. Build a reusable **read-only input adapter** that can consume actual `VehicleOdometry` callbacks when a transport exists, preserves supplied sample/publication/receipt identity, and refuses any observed reset-counter change or source/session change. This stage does not launch DDS, grant camera calibration, build `Observation`, append a training sequence, or change flight control.

## Pinned upstream contract and choice

PX4 `d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4` is BSD-3-Clause. The local `VehicleOdometry.msg` SHA-256 is `a528b3d0b4c1a9083a71b32c367bad71a900e959efd82b9d03a9eb0e99fe6017`; `dds_topics.yaml` SHA-256 is `7e9c730d45b22af92acfebbb81cb5de4e4a69ac0ca46b012b71ed8fa5c89cbc0`; `EKF2.cpp` SHA-256 is `1984b373bb7712dcdf1dba6bc8efb1511e3aeebcf7da3a08bd2738fac790df06`. These three files are unmodified in the local checkout, although other PX4 files may differ. The message supplies position, quaternion, velocity, angular velocity, variance, sample/publication time and one 8-bit reset counter. A complete wrap between observed callbacks can alias the same value and is not detectable by this field alone; independent source continuity remains required. Fixed EKF2 publishes NED pose and velocity, sets quality to zero, and forms odometry reset_counter from attitude, position and velocity reset counts. Its quality field is explicitly unused, so zero cannot mean healthy. The fixed DDS list includes `/fmu/out/vehicle_odometry`. [Pinned message](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/blob/d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4/msg/versioned/VehicleOdometry.msg); [PX4 DDS documentation](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/middleware/uxrce_dds). The checkout is an older pinned revision; present upstream activity does not prove this installation is unmodified at runtime.

Reuse NumPy/SciPy already in the project for explicit NED→ENU and FRD camera geometry. A separate ROS 2/uXRCE subscriber would require an Agent and message bindings not installed in this WSL environment; this stage accepts callback records without pretending to own a live transport. MAVLink `LOCAL_POSITION_NED` lacks the same reset and receipt evidence and is not substituted. Existing Gazebo camera geometry is only a simulator candidate, not hardware extrinsic calibration.

## Input and output contract

`VehicleOdometryEvent` carries the exact DDS topic name, explicit estimator session and topic instance, sample and publication microseconds, callback receipt monotonic nanoseconds, PX4 frame enums, position, Hamilton body-FRD→NED quaternion, velocity, body angular velocity, variance triples, `reset_counter`, and unused `quality`. The caller still must later attest transport/process identity; strings alone are not proof. Reject wrong topic, unsupported frame enums, malformed shapes/types, nonfinite values, bad quaternion norm, invalid variance, nonpositive/nonmonotonic clocks and sample later than publication. Keep at most 256 events and a permanent failure latch for changed session, topic instance, observed reset-counter change, time reversal or a source sample gap above 100,000 microseconds. Session identifiers are nonempty opaque strings and instance identifiers are nonnegative integers; neither is treated as authenticated by this module. Do not silently start a new coordinate epoch.

Given strictly increasing camera sample timestamps and callback receipts from the **same declared domains**, choose only an odometry publication no later than the camera sample whose sample is no later than the camera sample and whose receipt is no later than camera receipt. A duplicate or reversed camera sample or receipt time permanently latches the adapter. Refuse missing data or more than 100,000 microseconds of sample age or 100,000,000 nanoseconds of receipt age. Produce an immutable diagnostic candidate with validated numeric source coefficients and event identity (a separate normalized quaternion is used only for geometry; wire bytes are not retained), ENU position/velocity, ENU yaw/yaw rate and an ENU optical camera pose composed from a supplied body-FRD extrinsic. Reject yaw-rate geometry whose horizontal body-forward projection norm is below 0.1. The supplied extrinsic is camera-optical-to-body-FRD Hamilton quaternion and camera origin in body-FRD metres, with SHA-256 retained only as provenance, not a calibration grant. This transform proves arithmetic only: the extrinsic, clock epoch, EKF2 primary instance/health, live teacher, and goal-frame policy remain unqualified. Every candidate has `eligible_for_live_capture=false`; no helper converts it into `CaptureInputEvidence` or invokes the recorder.

## Tests and exit

Use synthetic records to verify NED/ENU and tilted quaternion/lever-arm geometry, yaw-rate against finite rotation, no-future sample/publication/receipt selection, wrong source/frame/shape, session or reset change, counter wrap, clock regression and stale/missing refusal. Run adjacent capture and reset audits, changed-file lint and independent review. A fixed development ULog may be inspected without constructing a corpus, but its prior reset failure remains. Seal all failures and hashes. The next stage is a real subscriber plus independent camera/clock/goal calibration and source-health attestation; only then may the checked recorder be considered.
